SLIDE 2 -- THE BUSINESS PROBLEM
Brands allocate large budgets to influencer marketing without a systematic way to predict
which influencers will deliver high return on investment before committing spend.
This pipeline replaces intuition-based influencer selection with data-driven ROI prediction,
automated influencer tier scoring, and a rules-based campaign intelligence engine.

SLIDE 7 -- FEATURE ENGINEERING: THE 12 PERFORMANCE SIGNALS
roi_ratio                    : Revenue/Cost — fundamental campaign efficiency metric
cost_per_discount_use        : Cost per tracked conversion — spend efficiency
revenue_per_follower         : Revenue yield per audience member — cuts vanity metrics
conversion_rate              : % followers who used discount — persuasion power
engagement_to_cost_efficiency: Engagement x Conversions / Cost — combined quality signal
audience_quality_score       : Composite 0-1 quality index normalised across platforms
influencer_experience_score  : Log(collabs) — commercial reliability proxy
revenue_per_comment          : Comment-to-revenue conversion — intent signal
cost_efficiency_tier         : Elite/Good/Fair/Poor cost bucket label
follower_tier                : Micro/Macro/Mega segmentation
engagement_tier              : Elite/Healthy/Low engagement label
platform_niche_combo         : Channel x Vertical interaction feature
SLIDE 8 -- ROI PREDICTION MODEL
Model ready: True
Algorithm: Gradient Boosting Classifier (best of LR / RF / GBM by AUC)
Task: Predict probability of High ROI before campaign spend is committed
Input: 14 influencer profile and performance features
Output: Binary High/Low ROI prediction + probability score (0-1)
Use case: Campaign managers score new influencers before contracting
SLIDE 9 -- REVENUE FORECASTING MODEL
Model ready: True
Algorithm: Gradient Boosting Regressor
Task: Forecast expected revenue from a campaign given influencer profile and budget
Key safety: roi_ratio excluded from features to prevent data leakage
Use case: Budget approval workflow — present predicted revenue range to finance team
